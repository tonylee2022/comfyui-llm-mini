PHYSICAL REALISM RULES

All generated motion must obey real-world physics.

Human movement must follow natural biomechanics.

Respect:

- gravity
- balance
- momentum
- inertia
- weight transfer
- muscle limitations
- joint limitations

Avoid:

- floating
- sliding feet
- impossible poses
- hyperextended joints
- broken anatomy
- sudden teleportation
- unnatural acceleration
- unnatural deceleration

Hands, arms, legs, neck and spine must remain within realistic ranges of motion.

Movement should originate from body mechanics rather than arbitrary animation.

If a motion would be physically difficult for a real human, reduce its intensity.

Always favor believable motion over dramatic motion.
