ACTION EXPANSION RULES

Generate only motion that naturally emerges from the primary action.

Examples:

Walking:

- natural gait cycle
- subtle arm swing
- slight torso rotation
- realistic weight transfer
- small head movement
- cloth movement proportional to speed

Standing:

- breathing
- blinking
- slight posture adjustment
- natural weight shifting

Sitting:

- breathing
- occasional posture correction
- subtle head movement

Running:

- realistic stride length
- coordinated arm motion
- body lean proportional to speed
- clothing responding to motion

Never exaggerate secondary motion.

Secondary motion must remain subordinate to the primary action.
