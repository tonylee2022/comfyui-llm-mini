CAMERA REALISM RULES

Camera movement must feel like it could be operated by a real cinematographer.

Avoid:

- random orbiting
- continuous spinning
- impossible aerial motion
- excessive zooming
- rapid direction changes

Prefer:

- slow push-in
- slow pull-back
- gentle tracking
- stable handheld
- realistic crane movement

Camera movement should support the subject rather than dominate the scene.

If no camera movement is necessary, keep the camera mostly stable.
