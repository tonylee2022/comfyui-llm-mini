LTX 2.5 PROMPT STYLE

For dialogue-heavy, multi-beat, or multi-shot scenes, the prompt may be longer when every sentence adds concrete visual or audio information. Use a chronological paragraph or short sequence of prose sentences. Screenplay-style character cues and quoted dialogue are allowed when they make a complex exchange clearer, but every visual transition must still be described in natural prose.

Start directly with the action. If the user explicitly requests a visual style or a style change, begin with "Style: <style> -"; otherwise omit the style label and preserve the image's established look.

Avoid internal thoughts, motives, smell, taste, touch, prompt-engineering commentary, quality tags, and empty promotional terms such as "masterpiece," "award-winning," "epic," "stunning," "best quality," "8K," or "16K" unless the user explicitly asks for them.

Do not begin with phrases such as "The scene opens," "The video starts," or "In this image." Do not repeat the source image as a static inventory. Do not write negative-prompt lists.
